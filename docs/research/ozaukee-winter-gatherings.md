# Where Ozaukee County gathers, November 2026 through March 2027

Research for issue #30, part of the winter lead-gen operating system map (issue #27).

**Research date: August 5, 2026.** Every "not published" note below means not published *as of that date*.

**Scope:** Cedarburg 53012, Mequon 53092 and 53097, Thiensville, Grafton 53024, Port Washington 53074, Saukville 53080. Plus a small number of adjacent-county items that Ozaukee residents attend, flagged as such.

**This document reports terrain only.** No recommendation about which opportunities to pick. That is a separate ticket.

---

## How to read the confidence labels

Every entry carries one of these:

- **CONFIRMED 2026-27** means the organizer's own site publishes the date for this coming season.
- **PRIOR YEAR** means only a 2025-26 or older date is published. The event exists, the date is a guess, and the guess is not mine to make.
- **NOT FOUND** means I checked a named page and the fact was not on it. It does not mean the fact does not exist.
- **UNREACHABLE** means the page returned 403, 404, or would not render. Named so it can be retried by phone or browser.

Three structural facts shape everything below:

1. **Most Ozaukee organizers have not published their 2026-27 winter calendars yet.** Every chamber calendar I loaded stopped at August 2026. This is normal for early August and it is the honest state of the terrain, not a research failure.
2. **The recurring cadence is the plannable fact.** Where a group meets "second Wednesday, September through May," that is worth more than an unpublished date.
3. **Two research paths were closed off.** The session's web search budget was exhausted mid-research, and several platforms (ClubRunner, which hosts every Rotary site in the county, and BNI's regional member search) are Cloudflare-blocked or broken. Gaps from those are named in the gaps section rather than papered over.
