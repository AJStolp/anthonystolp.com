## What the numbers say

Realtor.com's Cedarburg key indicators, as of November 2026, put the median listing price at $499,900, down 6.11% from the prior month and 15.26% from a year earlier. There were 56 active listings, up 35.90% from the prior month and 26.19% from a year earlier. The median time a listing had been on the market was 31 days, shorter than the prior month but 40.91% longer than a year earlier. Homes that sold were coming in at 98% of asking, or about 1.53% below the list price, and the median sold price was $508,000.

A November 5, 2026 update by Dillon Kanitz-Reed of Coldwell Banker Realty, based on Metro MLS statistics for Cedarburg, fills in October's closed sales. Sixteen homes sold, 27 new listings came on, and 40 homes were available, or 2.5 months of supply compared with 1.47 months year to date. The median sale price was $497,950, compared with $576,390 year to date. Homes that closed spent an average of 24 days on the market and sold for 99.59% of list price, compared with 102.02% year to date. With only 16 sales, a softer October median is not proof that every Cedarburg home lost value.

Zillow's Home Value Index for Cedarburg, updated October 31, 2026, was $558,988, up 6.4% over the prior year. That is an estimate of typical values, not a median of last month's sales.

Countywide, the Wisconsin REALTORS Association October 2026 report, released November 19, is the official statewide series I trust for Ozaukee County. In October, the median sale price was $475,000, down 2.3% from $485,950 a year earlier. Sales were 97, compared with 98. Months of inventory held at 3.8. Year to date through October, the median was $505,000, up 5.2% from $479,900, and sales were 1,043, up 11.8% from 933.

The Greater Milwaukee Association of REALTORS, using Metro MLS data in its November 13, 2026 release, counted Ozaukee a little differently (monthly totals are cut off on the 10th of the following month): 103 October sales, up 8.4% from 95, and 150 listings, up 23.0% from 122. Metro MLS Monthly Metrics for October put the four county median (Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Washington, and Waukesha) at $374,030, up 1.4%, with 2.6 months of supply and 98.2% of original list price received. The same WRA report puts the average 30 year fixed mortgage rate at 7.33% in October 2026, up from 6.25% in October 2025.

## What it means for sellers

This is not a market where you can pick a number and wait. Buyers have more homes to walk through than they did a year ago, and October closings came in a hair under asking after a year to date stretch above it. Pricing has to be honest, tied to what actually sold nearby, not to last spring's headlines. A well prepared, well priced home can still move in a few weeks. A home priced for a hotter month will sit, and sitting is what starts the price cuts.

## What it means for buyers

You have more choices than you did earlier this year. Compare the [homes for sale in Cedarburg](/search/cedarburg-homes-for-sale), look at how long a home has been listed, and do not be shy about asking for repairs or a credit when the condition or the price is off. The 98% sale to list figure from Realtor.com for November, and the 99.59% Metro MLS figure for October in Cedarburg, both say there is a little room to talk. That is not an invitation to lowball a sharp listing and hope it sits. Good homes in the Cedarburg School District still go quickly. A 7.33% average 30 year fixed rate in October is a real headwind. If you find the right house, talk with your lender about the payment today and what a later refinance would look like.

## A note on winter timing

Cedarburg gets quieter after the holidays, and that is not a bad time to buy or to get a home ready to list in late winter. If you need to move before spring, a winter listing can still work. The WRA's Tom Larson noted in the October report that sellers who list in the colder months tend to be more motivated, which can mean more room on the final price. The flip side is that a sharp, well presented listing has less competition in December than it will in April. If you want to be on the market when spring buyers show up, January and February are when we should talk about prep, pricing, and photos.
