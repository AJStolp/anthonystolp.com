## What's verified as of Nov. 27, 2026

- The county plans to offer the Cedarburg Firemen's Association $2 million, the asking price, for Firemen's Park, about 16.18 acres north of downtown Cedarburg, including the old concrete grandstand. With the county's land next door, it forms the 39 acre site for the free county fair every August. (Ozaukee Press, Nov. 24, 2026; Nov. 30 Executive Committee agenda packet)
- County Administrator Jason Dzwinel said the Executive Committee would consider the $2 million resolution on Monday, Nov. 30, and the County Board was likely to approve the offer on Wednesday, Dec. 2. Chairman Lee Schlenvogt and Dzwinel would not guess at a closing date. A county memo puts grandstand demolition and site work at about another $3 million. Schlenvogt said the focus is buying the land first. The fire department told the county the land would likely be worth more to a developer, but it would rather keep the fair where it is. The county has a right of first refusal; the City of Cedarburg has the next opportunity before an open-market sale. (Ozaukee Press, Nov. 24, 2026)

That's about 16 acres, including the old concrete grandstand.
